How to read these numbers

  • Do not rank them. Studies count differently. Some divide by every scheduled visit, others only by completed and missed visits, and late cancellations are sometimes counted separately.
  • Most are single sites. A study from one academic or safety-net system describes that system, not its whole specialty.
  • Population and visit type matter. One safety-net center reports 25% for in-person visits and 12% for telemedicine, so compare against a similar clinic.
  • The pandemic is in some periods. Check the dates before treating a figure as current.
  • Bars share a 0% to 90% axis. The lighter band is a range or a confidence interval, where the source gives one. The hollow marker is a target, not a measurement.

What is included

A figure is listed when the source states the rate directly and says what population it describes. Each was checked against the abstract, full text or publisher page named in its row on 2026-11-05. Nothing here is modeled, blended or averaged by us. This is a selection, not a systematic review. Physical therapy, oncology and general dentistry have no measured row yet, because the sources we found were small, or we could not read them.
